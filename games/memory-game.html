🃏 Memory Card Flip

Uncover pairs using 3D perspective flips and test your recall ability.

Rules and Regulations

Objective: Match all the pairs of cards.

Rules

  1. 12 cards (6 pairs) are placed face down.
  2. Click a card to flip it, then click a second card.
  3. If the two cards match, they stay face up.
  4. If they don't match, they flip back after 1 second.
  5. Matching all pairs wins the game. Your number of moves is shown at the end, and fewer moves is better.

Regulations

  • Only two cards can be open at a time.
  • A card that is already matched cannot be clicked again.
  • You cannot click the same card twice in one turn.
  • Cards are shuffled at the start of every new game.
