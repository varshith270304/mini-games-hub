🔢 Number Guessing

Can you figure out the mystery number chosen by the computer?

Rules and Regulations

Objective: Guess the secret number chosen by the computer.

Rules

  1. The computer picks a random number between 1 and 100.
  2. You have 7 attempts to guess it.
  3. After each guess you are told whether it is too high or too low.
  4. Guess the number within 7 attempts to win; otherwise you lose and the number is revealed.
  5. Click "Play Again" to start a new round.

Regulations

  • Only whole numbers from 1 to 100 are accepted.
  • An empty input, text, or a number outside the range shows an error message and does not use up an attempt.
  • Each round has a new secret number.
